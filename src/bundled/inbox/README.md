# Inbox

Brings your mentions, direct messages, thread replies, and reactions to your messages together in one place, so you can catch up quickly.

## Getting started

1. Open **Inbox** in the sidebar.
2. Select a conversation to read and reply without leaving Inbox.
3. Use **Inbox filters** to focus on **Threads**, **Mentions**, **Reactions**, or **DMs**, or to find your **Drafts**.
